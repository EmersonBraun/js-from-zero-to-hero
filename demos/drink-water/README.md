# Drink Water

> [pt-br version](README-PTBR.md)

A water tracking app that helps you stay hydrated with visual feedback, reminders, and progress tracking.

![Drink Water](gifs/class.gif)

## CLASS

### Features

- **Water Tracking**: Track daily water consumption with visual cup
- **Customizable Goals**: Set your own daily water intake goal
- **Glass Size Control**: Adjust glass size to match your containers
- **Visual Progress**: See progress with animated water cup and filled glasses
- **Reminder System**: Get notifications to drink water at set intervals
- **Daily Statistics**: View glasses consumed, total ml, and goal progress
- **History Tracking**: Save and view your hydration history
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices

### How to Use

1. **Set Your Goal**: Adjust daily water intake goal (default: 2000ml)
2. **Choose Glass Size**: Set your preferred glass size (default: 250ml)
3. **Drink Water**: Click "Drink Water" to consume from your cup
4. **Refill Cup**: Click "Refill Cup" when your cup is empty
5. **Track Progress**: Watch your daily progress and filled glasses
6. **Set Reminders**: Choose reminder intervals to stay hydrated

### Controls

- **Drink Water**: Consume water from your cup (25% at a time)
- **Refill Cup**: Fill your cup back to 100%
- **Glass Clicking**: Click glasses to add/remove them from your count
- **Reset Day**: Start fresh for a new day
- **View History**: See your past hydration records

### Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Spacebar`: Drink water
- `Ctrl+R`: Refill cup
- `Escape`: Reset day

### Daily Goal Recommendations

- **Adults**: 2000-3000ml (8-12 glasses)
- **Active individuals**: 3000-4000ml (12-16 glasses)
- **Hot weather**: Add 500-1000ml extra

### Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3 (with animations and visual effects)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Local Storage API
- Google Fonts (Roboto)
